## Remaining: 3

**Three hypotheses remain for the lifetime of this project.** S1 is the first. When the budget reaches zero
the search ends and the project concludes with whatever it has, including nothing.

Rules for spending one:
1. The idea comes from **published research or an economic argument that does not come from looking at this
   data.** An idea generated by examining the data is not eligible, ever.
2. It is **pre-registered and hashed before any data is examined for it**, including exploratory charts.
3. The bar is **t ≥ 3.5** or higher. It does not fall.
4. It must state in advance what result would make it fail, and the failure must be published.
5. If it needs new paid data, `scripts/preflight_coverage.py` runs first (`docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`). No
   further paid data is currently planned.
6. Re-testing a rejected hypothesis, in any re-parameterised form, on this data is **prohibited** and does not
   cost a budget line because it is not permitted at all.

## How the project ends

It ends in one of three ways, all of which are acceptable and all of which get published:

1. **A survivor passes its forward test.** H8 under `PREREG_H8_FORWARD.md`, or S1 under an equivalent forward
   pre-registration. Forward evidence on unseen events is the only thing that promotes anything.
2. **The budget is spent with no survivor.** The project concludes with *no demonstrated trading edge*, and
   that is written up as the result. This is the most likely outcome and it is not a failure of the work.
3. **A survivor is retired as unresolvable.** H8 already has this branch written into its forward
   pre-registration: positive but short of the bar at 40 events means retirement, not indefinite extension.

No real money is committed under any branch without satisfying the prerequisites in
`PREREG_H8_FORWARD.md` §6 in full.
