## 2. Defect one: contract rolls

Databento continuous symbols splice quarterly contracts **without back-adjustment**, so the series jumps by
the calendar spread at each roll — positive for equity index futures, ~1% a quarter at current rates. A
trader holding one contract never experiences that jump. Every affected strategy here is long, so the
contamination only inflates.

It hit H8 **systematically, not by luck**: quarterly rolls land in the third week of March, June, September
and December, and so do FOMC meetings.

| | Holds crossing a roll | t published | t roll-adjusted | Bar | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H8 MNQ training | 4–5 of 48 | 3.02 | **2.47–2.63** | 3.0 | **PASS → FAIL** |
| H8 MES replication | 11 of 59 | 3.56 | **1.66** | 2.0 | **PASS → FAIL** |
| H8 M2K replication | 12 of 59 | 2.45 | **1.44** | 2.0 | **PASS → FAIL** |
| H8 gold control | 0 of 55 | 0.59 | 0.59 | — | none |
| All other hypotheses | 0–1 each | — | ≤0.03 change | — | none |

**The eleven failures stand. The one pass does not.** Detail: `AUDIT_2026_11_ROLLS.md`.

The cause was a docstring asserting the risk did not apply — true when written, when the bot was
intraday-only, and false once overnight families were added. Nobody revisited it.

## 4. Corrected PBO

CSCV per Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu (2017). The engine was **independently verified**: a separate
naive implementation reproduced identical λ vectors to 1e-9 and every figure to four decimals.

| | All 12 | **Intraday (8)** | Event (4) | Winner's Sharpe IS → OOS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| As published | 8.2% | **56.6%** | 18.5% | +0.074 → +0.051 |
| Roll-adjusted | **11.3%** | **56.6%** | 25.5% | +0.068 → **+0.038** |
| Range across S and trim direction | **6.5–20.2%** | 51–61% | — | — |

**The finding that belongs in the final conclusions:** among the eight intraday strategies — where most of
this project's effort went — selecting the in-sample best lands below the out-of-sample median **56.6%** of
the time, with a **negative** out-of-sample Sharpe. That converts "eleven intraday hypotheses failed" from an
observation into a measurement, and it is robust across every variation tested.

The overall figure is **a range, not a point estimate**. It is also **not** support for H8: PBO is computed
wholly within 2019-05 → 2025-06 and structurally cannot see the holdout, which failed.

Supporting measurements: daily Sharpe tracks per-trade t at correlation **0.9997**, so pooling strategies of
very different trade frequencies is legitimate; mean pairwise correlation is 0.036 and the effective number
of independent trials is **11.33 of 12**, so correlation does **not** meaningfully soften the
multiple-testing thresholds.

| Test | Result | Reading |

| Placebo, sham dates | real window best of 7 in all 3 markets | window is special, but no longer significant alone |
| Nikkei replication | **INVALID** — 16 of 59 events | could not be run; says nothing either way |
| Multiple testing, N=12 | 5% familywise bar is **t ≥ 2.63** | H8 sits **on or under** that line |
| PBO | low, but blind to the holdout | no support either way |
| Forward paper events | **0 so far**; first Dec 8–9 | the only clean evidence available |

**Status: unconfirmed, below its original bar, and sitting on the multiple-testing line.** Not a candidate
strategy — a reason to keep collecting forward events.

The forward test's own arithmetic is sobering: on roll-adjusted effect size ($97.44/event, spread $318),
80% power needs **66 events ≈ 8.2 years**, and at 40 events the expected t is **1.94 against a 2.0 bar**.
The design is underpowered by construction. The bar was **not** lowered; the test's purpose was changed to a
falsification check whose realistic value is killing H8 early.

## 7. Execution costs — not load-bearing

No stored fill records exist (drill journals were not retained), so this is a proxy analysis from OHLCV and
says so. One hard datum: observed commission **$0.61/side against $0.62 assumed** — slightly conservative.

1 tick is a **fair** model of the spread (MNQ quotes one tick wide in these hours; a one-lot market order
fills at the touch) but **optimistic about timing** (entry minutes span 23–91 ticks of range). It changes
nothing: **quintupling H8's slippage moves its t by 0.08.** The roll correction moved it ten times further.
Detail: `EXECUTION_COST_AUDIT.md`.

## 9. The evidence a strategy now needs before it may be called validated

All of the following, with no exceptions and no post-hoc amendments:

1. **Pre-registered and hashed** before any data for it is examined, including exploratory charts.
2. **Priced on the actual contract held**, or on a properly back-adjusted series. No unadjusted continuous
   series for any strategy that holds overnight.
3. **Passes the data-integrity gate** — totals reconciled to source, no dates lost, zero distinguished from
   missing, contract identity recorded.
4. **t ≥ 3.5** on its own evidence, which is above the 1% familywise threshold of 3.14 for the trials run.
5. **Beats a benchmark that controls for market drift** — a random same-length long hold. Five of this
   project's failures made money and would have been reported as successes without this.
6. **Survives a placebo test** — the same rule on sham dates must not reproduce it.
7. **Positive after doubling modelled slippage**, and positive **at zero cost** (a result that only exists
   because of a cost assumption is not an edge).
8. **Passes a clean out-of-sample test** on data untouched when the rule was fixed. H8's failure here is the
   single most informative result in the project.
9. **Forward paper events on unseen data**, under a pre-registered forward protocol with a stated futility
   stop — and the decisive evidence, never backtests alone.
10. **Prerequisites before any real money**: margin account, position sized so one bad event is ≤2% of
    capital, every risk control verified live, and a written answer to *what would make me stop?*

Any strategy failing any one of these is **not validated**, however good it looks.
